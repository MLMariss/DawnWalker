Plan a build for The Blood of Dawnwalker before you spend a skill point. This planner lays out all three skill trees — Witchcraft, Swordmastery and Vampirism — with their 54 perks, 9 ultimate perks and 27 abilities. It follows prerequisites and corruption gates, totals the skill points, time segments and manuals a build needs, and saves any build as a link you can share.

Witchcraft skill tree

Witchcraft perks

  • Forager (2 levels, any time) Double Harvest chance when gathering plants.
  • Bewitching Influence (3 levels, any time) Better Prices and special offers in stores.
  • Unnatural Resilience (2 levels, day only) Grants Armour when any Witchcraft Ability is active.
  • Witchcraft Mastery (4 levels, day only) Witchcraft Abilities deal more Damage.
  • Herbal Remedies I (3 levels, any time) Consumables with duration measured in time segments last longer.
  • Medicus I (3 levels, any time) Increases the limit of Consumables in a Quickslot.
  • Forbidden Sigils (3 levels, day only) Increases Witchcraft Ability Slots number.
  • Lasting Malediction (4 levels, day only) Witchcraft Abilities last longer.
  • Frugal Witchcraft (3 levels, day only) Defeating an enemy with a Witchcraft Ability restores Health based on the amount spent to cast it.
  • Amalgam (2 levels, any time) Multiple Buffs from Consumables with time segments set as the duration, can be applied simultaneously.
  • Aether Flow (3 levels, day only) Reduces Witchcraft Ability Cooldown.
  • Evil Eye (2 levels, day only) Chance for Critical Witchcraft Ability cast.
  • Font of Life (1 level, day only, story unlock) Passive regeneration of human health.
  • Herbal Remedies II (3 levels, any time) Additional instant Heal from all Consumables.
  • Medicus II (3 levels, any time) Craft more Items in a single batch.
  • Blasphemous Echo (2 levels, day only) Increases Witchcraft Abilities Damage for each enemy currently affected by Witchcraft Abilities.
  • Potent Blood (2 levels, day only) Chance for Witchcraft Abilities to not cost Health.
  • Unholy Fervour (2 levels, day only) Chance for a refund of an Activation Charge after Witchcraft Ability use.

Witchcraft ultimate perks

Three per tree, only one can ever be taken; the choice is permanent.

  • Aether Cascade (also listed as Arcane Cascade) +20% Witchcraft damage for each ability used, up to +100%. Resets after combat. Requires 35 points in tree; 4 skill points, 2 time segments.
  • Entwined Torment The first Witchcraft ability used in a fight hits 2 enemies. Requires 35 points in tree; 4 skill points, 2 time segments.
  • Runic Bulwark 15% chance to apply a random hex on a perfect block. Requires 35 points in tree; 4 skill points, 2 time segments.

Witchcraft abilities

  • Astral Communion (passive, 4 levels, story granted) Activates dormant magic in places of power scattered throughout the Valley.
  • Burning Blood (active, 4 levels, story granted) Deals Damage over time to a target.
  • Compel Soul (passive, 4 levels, story granted) Allows you to speak with restless souls. Use together with objects associated with the dead to obtain additional information.
  • Cycle of Ruin (passive, 4 levels) Extends Duration of currently active Witchcraft Abilities with each landed Attack, Block and Perfect Block.
  • Life Lock (active, 4 levels) Blocks Damage and Reflects it back to the enemy.
  • Mercurial Fervour (passive, 4 levels) Gain Haste in exploration by briefly slowing down the world around you.
  • Ravenous Flock (active, 4 levels) Area Attack and Stun on target. Attacking the target triggers additional Area Attacks.
  • Soul Reaping (active, 4 levels) Lifesteals target over time and keep him away.
  • Soul Stigma (active, 4 levels) Higher Critical Chance and Critical Damage on target.
  • Unholy Vitality (passive, 4 levels) Regenerates Health after using Active Abilities.

Swordmastery skill tree

Swordmastery perks

  • Omniblock (4 levels, any time) Omniblock costs less Stamina.
  • Stinging Blade (4 levels, any time) Increases Weapon Damage.
  • Endless Effort (4 levels, any time) Increases Stamina.
  • Vigour (4 levels, any time) Increases All Health Types.
  • Perfect Riposte (4 levels, any time) Attack in Critical Direction after Perfect Block to deal Critical Damage.
  • Perfect Block (4 levels, any time) Perfect Block restores more Stamina and Activation Charge.
  • Fate’s Favour (4 levels, any time) Chance for Weapon Critical Hit.
  • Fleet of Foot (4 levels, any time) Dodging cost less Stamina.
  • Pack Mule (3 levels, any time) Increases Carry Weight.
  • Sustained Focus (4 levels, any time) Increases Activation Charges number.
  • Second Skin I (3 levels, any time) Decreases Medium Armour penalties.
  • Counterattack (3 levels, any time) Attack after Perfect Block deals more Damage.
  • Precision (3 levels, any time) Deal more Damage with Directional Attacks when directions don't repeat.
  • Master Fencer (3 levels, any time) Increases Swordmastery Ability Slots number.
  • Renewed Focus (3 levels, any time) Increases Activation Charge Restoration.
  • Sharp Eye (3 levels, any time) Chance to Weaken enemy on Perfect Block. Weak enemy deals less and receives more Damage.
  • Growing Momentum (2 levels, any time) Increases Weapon Damage with each Attack. Buff resets after receiving Damage.
  • Restless Blade (3 levels, any time) Reduces Cooldowns of Swordmastery Abilities.
  • Second Skin II (3 levels, any time) Decreases Heavy Armour penalties.

Swordmastery ultimate perks

Three per tree, only one can ever be taken; the choice is permanent.

  • Last Stand +100% Weapon Damage when Health is below 30%. Requires 35 points in tree; 4 skill points, 2 time segments.
  • Sword Sage Reset Active Ability Cooldowns on enemy killing. Requires 35 points in tree; 4 skill points, 2 time segments.
  • Tactical Mastery -1 Activation Charge cost to all Active Abilities (minimum 1). Requires 35 points in tree; 4 skill points, 2 time segments.

Swordmastery abilities

  • Adrenaline Rush (passive, 4 levels) Increases Attack Damage after using Active Abilities.
  • Artery Strike (active, 4 levels) Critical Attack with a chance of Decapitating the target.
  • Broad Swing (active, 4 levels) Weapon Area Attack.
  • Charge (active, 4 levels) Charge dealing Damage and Stunning a target and everyone on your way.
  • Dirty Trick (active, 4 levels, story granted) Stuns and Damages a target and makes them vulnerable to Active Abilities.
  • Swiftness (passive, 4 levels) Boosts Attack Speed after Perfect Block.
  • Walking Fortress (passive, 4 levels) Perfect Block restores portion of Activation Charge.

Vampirism skill tree

Vampirism perks

  • Nourishing Blood (3 levels, night only) Blood Drinking restores more Health.
  • Endless Hunger (3 levels, night only) Increase Health Segments number.
  • Shadow Dweller (2 levels, night only) Increases Shadowstep range.
  • Razorsharp Claws (4 levels, night only) Increases Claw Damage.
  • Vrakhiri Might (3 levels, night only) Increases Vampiric Ability Slots number.
  • Crimson Feast (3 levels, night only) Grants Buffs when drinking Human Blood. Duration: 3 time segments.
  • Wild Blood (3 levels, night only) Grants Buffs when drinking Animal Blood. Duration: 3 time segments.
  • Dimension Reach (3 levels, night only) Decrease Planeshift Stamina cost.
  • Restless Claws (3 levels, night only) Decrease Stamina cost of Claw Attacks.
  • Lasting Blood (2 levels, night only) Vampiric Abilities cost less Health.
  • Hastened Corruption (3 levels, night only) Corruption fills up faster.
  • Closing Wounds (2 levels, night only) Slowly Regenerates Vampiric Health within the current Health Segment.
  • Mandrake Ward (1 level, night only, story unlock) Once per night, lethal damage instead restores 3 health segments.
  • Clawpierce (3 levels, night only) Chance for Claw Critical Hit.
  • Endless Ferocity (2 levels, night only) Reduces Cooldowns of Vampiric Abilities.
  • Blood Theft (3 levels, night only) Attacking restores Recently Lost Vampiric Health.
  • Dancing Claws (2 levels, night only) Increases Claw Critical Chance with each Attack. Buff resets after receiving Damage or after a Critical Hit.

Vampirism ultimate perks

Three per tree, only one can ever be taken; the choice is permanent.

  • Lethal Crescendo +20% Claw Damage after killing an enemy or depleting a Health Segment of a Boss. Can stack. Resets at the end of combat. Requires Corruption 15; 4 skill points, 2 time segments.
  • Renounce Death Gain Lifesteal on Attacks and Immortality instead of dying once per combat. Duration 10s. Requires Corruption 15; 4 skill points, 2 time segments.
  • Sanguine Renewal Voracious Bite resets all Active Abilities Cooldowns. Requires Corruption 15; 4 skill points, 2 time segments.

Vampirism abilities

  • Blood Surge (active, 4 levels) Explodes target. Bosses receive Damage instead.
  • Crimson Rush (passive, 4 levels) Increases Damage of Active Abilities, the higher your Health percentage is.
  • Death From Above (passive, 4 levels) Instantly Kill a target below you. Bosses receive Damage instead.
  • Mesmerise (active, 4 levels) Mind Control a target and force him to Fight on your side. Can't be used on Bosses.
  • Piercing Shriek (active, 4 levels) Stuns all enemies in front of you, Weakens their Defence and Attack Damage. Blocking their attacks reflects Damage back at them.
  • Scarlet Shield (passive, 4 levels) Health Regeneration chance after Block.
  • Shadowstorm (active, 4 levels) Slows down Time around you.
  • Shapeshift (passive, 4 levels) Shapeshift into a Wolf and gain Haste in exploration.
  • Shred (passive, 4 levels) Bleed chance on your Weapon or Claw Attacks.
  • Voracious Bite (active, 4 levels, story granted) Restores Health by drinking target's blood.
