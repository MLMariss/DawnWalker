The Blood of Dawnwalker Skill Tree Planner

Free planner for the Witchcraft, Swordmastery and Vampirism trees, with every level’s cost and gate read from the game’s own skill screens. Full perk list

Ultimate Perks only one per skill tree, permanently
Abilities
Perk & Ability Reference every perk, ultimate and ability in one list

Every perk, ultimate perk and ability in The Blood of Dawnwalker’s three skill trees — 54 perks, 9 ultimates and 27 abilities — with each level’s effect, its skill point and time segment cost, and what gates it: a manual, a road shrine, a corruption level or a story quest.

Costs, gates and effect text were transcribed from the game’s own skill screens and cross-checked against Game8 and Fextralife; VERIFICATION.md lists every check and where the sources disagree. Damage figures are shown at character level 20. Made by MLMariss; the data and code are on GitHub.

Witchcraft ultimate perks

Three per tree, only one can ever be taken; the choice is permanent.

  • Aether Cascade (also listed as Arcane Cascade) +20% Witchcraft damage for each ability used, up to +100%. Resets after combat. Requires 35 points in tree; 4 skill points, 2 time segments.
  • Entwined Torment The first Witchcraft ability used in a fight hits 2 enemies. Requires 35 points in tree; 4 skill points, 2 time segments.
  • Runic Bulwark 15% chance to apply a random hex on a perfect block. Requires 35 points in tree; 4 skill points, 2 time segments.
